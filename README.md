# eBay Smart Seller Toolkit

A powerful, easy-to-use Chrome Extension designed to simplify your eBay selling experience. This toolkit provides actionable insights, sales data scraping, and intuitive on-page tools directly within your eBay Seller Hub.

## Features

- **Sales Data Dashboard:** Track and visualize your daily sales directly from the eBay All Orders page.
- **Image Downloader:** Download high-quality product images directly from eBay listings with one click.
- **Sold History Integrations:** Instantly check sold history for items from search pages, listings, carousels, and shop pages.
- **Analytics Sidebar:** Quickly view metrics and sales momentum on your purchase history pages.
- **Customizable Settings:** Enable or disable specific features based on your workflow through the extension popup.

## How to Install (Developer Mode)

1. Download or clone this repository to your computer.
2. Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder containing this extension (`ebay-smart-seller-toolkit`).
5. Pin the extension to your toolbar for quick access!

## How to Use

1. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the popup.
2. Use the **Page Tools** tab to configure your preferred settings and features.
3. To analyze your orders, go to your eBay **Seller Hub > Orders** page, open the extension popup, and click **Scan Current Page**.
4. Click **Dashboard** to view your beautiful, organized sales metrics.

## Updates

This tool is provided as-is, but new updates, bug fixes, and improvements will be released from time to time to maintain compatibility with eBay.
